# DRAFT — not yet in src/content/blog, won't build or publish

## Working title
Why I'm Writing This

## One-line premise
Starting a personal site isn't the interesting part — using it as forcing
function to think and ship in public, in the small amount of time I actually
have, is.

## Outline

1. **The honest reason I'm doing this**
   Not "personal branding" as a category — a specific discomfort with being
   entirely dependent on one employer, and wanting some evidence (to myself,
   before anyone else) that I can build and ship things independently.

2. **What this site is and isn't**
   Not a content marketing funnel. Not a portfolio performance. A place to
   write short, honest posts about product management, career optionality,
   and whatever I'm building on the side — published imperfect rather than
   held back polished.

3. **The rule I'm setting for myself**
   Something ships here roughly as often as I have an evening to give it —
   not daily, not "whenever inspiration strikes." Consistency over
   intensity. Naming the cadence publicly is part of the commitment device.

4. **What's coming**
   A short, concrete list of 2-3 things I'm actually planning to write about
   next (fill in once decided) — gives the reader (and future me) a reason
   to check back.

## Notes to self before publishing
- Keep it under ~500 words. This is a "here's the shape of the thing" post,
  not an essay.
- Don't over-explain the four-paths framework or anything from the coaching
  side — this is a public post, keep it about the site and the habit.
- Decide the 2-3 "what's coming" items before publishing — don't leave it
  vague.
