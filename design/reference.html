Charging planner – component reference

Every component the PRD (§6.1) asks for, in every state, built from the themed AdminLTE 4 and Bootstrap 5.3 CSS (planner-theme.scss) and the Greener Miles tokens. Claude Code copies this markup. If a screen needs something that isn't here, it asks before inventing it (UI spec §1).

Fictional example: Greener Miles and its data are invented. Sample data is from PRD Appendix A, Tue 16 June 2026. Switch your device to dark mode to see the dark theme. Grey boxes, labels and notes like this one are reference scaffolding, not planner components.

Phone timeline C06 · SCR-02 · REQ-04 · DEC-78 to DEC-82

One slot per half hour, 07:00 to 16:00. The 07:00 slot lists every bike (DEC-40); later slots list only bikes that change, or "No changes". Each bike row links to its record. The current slot has a solid surface background with side padding, and its time is marked "now" with aria-current="time" (UI-A-01). Each slot with bikes listed is a native <details> (DEC-78): closed, it shows the time, band, how many bikes change and a chevron; open, the bike rows. Every slot is closed on load (DEC-79). A slot with no changes reads "No changes" and doesn't open. Following a band chart bar opens its slot. Above the band chart (C18): Bikes in and Bikes out now, in the asset summary card's facts (DEC-81), and the "Rest of day" and "All day" views in C08's segmented control (DEC-82). "Rest of day", the default, starts at the current slot, which lists every bike; after 16:30 it reads "Nothing more today". No print button on phones. Extract below: now is 07:00, so "Rest of day" is the whole day.

Band chart C18 · SCR-02 · SCR-03 · DEC-76 · DEC-80

Today's 19 slots, one bar each in its band's colour, at the top of the day plan above the slots or grid, whose band tags stay. Bar height is relative to the day's lowest and highest: no axis values, numbers other than the hours, tooltip, highlighted window or advice. On phones in "Rest of day" it draws only the slots from the current one (DEC-82). A slot with no forecast is a short hatched bar. Each whole hour is numbered under its bar in small text and "now" is marked in text; the band names are on the slots' band tags, not under the bars (DEC-80). A native <details> ("Carbon forecast"), open by default; each device remembers open or closed in the gm_bandchart cookie, and without JavaScript it's always open. Each bar links to its slot. Not printed. Switch your device to dark mode to see the dark theme: band colours don't change. Below: now is 12:10, open; and closed.

Buttons and action bar C15

One primary button per screen. Secondary actions are outlined. Cancel is a link. No delete button anywhere (DEC-09). On phones, form buttons sit in a bar that stays above the tab bar.

Labels

Verb plus thing: "Save swap", "Save fault", "Clear fault", "Save dealer service", "Add a battery". Not "Submit", "OK" or "Confirm".

Notices C16 · REQ-17 · PRD §7.3

Inline at the top of the page, never toasts or pop-ups. A save confirmation appears on the page the user lands on after saving, and says what changed.
